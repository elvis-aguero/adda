\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\conjecture{Venue-dependent, and left as a question rather than guessed at.
The three candidate framings and what each must defend are tabulated in
\texttt{paper/README.md}. This section is written last.}

What does not move with the venue is the shape of the problem. An autonomous
agent asked to do data-driven design can produce a fluent report of work it did
not do, and nothing in a transcript distinguishes that from the real thing. The
design decisions in \cref{sec:method} are all, in one way or another, answers
to that: they make the run's claims checkable by something other than the
agent's own account of them.

The register in \cref{sec:method} is the paper's centre of gravity. Each entry
names a mechanism, what it replaced, and what it costs. Entries whose cost is
asserted rather than measured are visible as such, which is the point of
writing it this way.
